WELCOME TO THE FACTORY

"Looks mechanical. Plays simple."

1 Your job is simple: Connect the gears and make the machine run.
2 The motor powers the first gear: It turns the entire machine.
3 Different gear sizes change speed: Big gears turn slower, small gears turn faster.
MECHANICAL BASICS

WHAT IS RPM?

RPM means how many times the gear turns in one minute (Revolutions Per Minute).

⚙
Small Gears = Spin Faster

Fewer teeth require less time per turn, producing higher speed.

⚙
Big Gears = Spin Slower

More teeth take more time per turn, producing lower speed.

🔄
Touching Gears Turn Opposite

When two gears mesh, one turns clockwise (↻) and the other turns counter-clockwise (↺).
